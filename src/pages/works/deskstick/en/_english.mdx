## Features

### Sync your stickies with iCloud.
Your stickies sync via iCloud, so the same notes are available on your Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Vision Pro. There is no per-device local database, so you never have to wonder which device holds the latest copy.

### Keep writing offline.
Edits you make on a plane or underground are kept on the device and pushed to iCloud automatically once you are back online. Losing connectivity never means losing what you were writing.

### Quick input from the menu bar (macOS)
Click the menu bar icon and a quick input field opens right there. Press `⌘⏎` and the note is saved and stuck to your desktop as a window. No app switching between the thought and writing it down.

### See everything in the organizer (macOS)
The organizer is a two-pane window: pick a note in the list on the left and edit or delete it directly in the pane on the right. Even with a lot of notes, you can skim through their contents without opening a window for each one.

### Always front
Notes can be pinned. A pinned note stays in front even while you work in another app. Use the pin icon, or toggle it with `⌘P`.

### Colorful and creative.
You can set the color of the note, and it will also be synchronized via iCloud — an instant way to tell "work", "personal", and "experimental" apart.

### Apple Intelligence summary titles (macOS 26 or later)
Turn this on in settings and a short summary of each note's contents appears as its window title and in the organizer list. The summarization happens on your Mac; the contents are never sent anywhere.

### Choose the menu bar, the Dock, or both (macOS)
The settings window (`⌘,`) lets you decide whether DeskStick lives in the menu bar, in the Dock, or both. At least one of the two is always shown, so the app can never disappear on you.

### Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets (iPhone / iPad)
Keep your latest notes on the Home Screen. There is a "sticky list" widget that shows several notes at once and a "single sticky" widget that shows one note you pick, both in small, medium, and large sizes. On the Lock Screen you can add a circular widget that starts a new note in one tap, plus a rectangular widget showing your most recent note. Tapping any widget opens that note directly in the app.

### List and grid views (iPhone / iPad)
Switch the overview between a list and a grid. Your choice is remembered the next time you launch the app.

## Supported devices and requirements

DeskStick is **available on the App Store** for Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Vision Pro. The macOS build can also be installed with Homebrew (`brew install --cask krgpi/tap/deskstick`). Notes sync seamlessly via iCloud, so you can start a prompt on your Mac and pick it up on your iPhone.

| | |
|------|------|
| Operating systems | macOS 13.0 or later / iOS and iPadOS 16.0 or later / visionOS |
| Requirements | An iCloud account signed in on the device, with iCloud Drive enabled |
| Sync | Through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). No sign-up, no separate account |
| Data handling | Nothing passes through the developer's servers. No ads, no tracking ([privacy policy](/works/deskstick/privacy/)) |

## Use cases for the AI era — keep your prompts and LLM outputs handy

DeskStick works particularly well as a lightweight companion for anyone who uses generative AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.) every day.

### A snippet board for prompts you reuse
Templates such as "code review prompt", "meeting notes summarizer", or "translate to English" can each live on their own sticky. They stay reachable while you bounce between browser tabs and chat apps, ready to be copied into the next AI conversation. Color coding makes it easy to separate work, personal, and experimental prompts at a glance.

### A draft pad for Slack, email, and chat messages
Before you hit send on something written in the heat of the moment, write it on a sticky and read it back — that one extra beat is what saves you from "I shouldn't have said that". Drafting on a sticky instead of directly in the recipient's input field also avoids accidental sends and misfires. Especially valuable for sensitive customer replies, reports to your manager, and anything you'll regret sending unedited.

### An always-pinned scratchpad for fleeting thoughts
A pinned sticky stays on top of every other app, which makes it the perfect always-on scratchpad for whatever pops into your head — meeting notes, a URL or number you want to keep nearby, a to-do you'll think about later. Having a "park it here for now" surface always visible in the corner of your screen lets you offload thoughts without breaking flow.

### A scratchpad for LLM outputs
When an AI returns a code snippet or a draft, you often want to compare a few versions before committing them to your editor or document. DeskStick acts as a **disposable scratchpad** for those LLM outputs — pin a sticky on top so it stays visible no matter which app is in focus.

### Cross-device AI workflows
Start drafting a prompt on your desk Mac and finish it on your MacBook — or your iPhone — on the go. Because notes sync via iCloud, the constant "save a fragment, pick it up somewhere else" rhythm of working with AI fits naturally with DeskStick.

### Compared to Apple's built-in Stickies
macOS ships with a "Stickies" app, but DeskStick adds **iCloud sync** and **menubar quick access**, both of which make it well-suited to high-frequency note taking around AI tools.

## How to use
### Select action from the Menubar (macOS)
  You can select "Show All Stickies" and "New Sticky" from the menubar.

![menubar](/stickies-menubar.png)

### The buttons of the sticky note

| Button | Function |
|--------|----------|
| X | Close the sticky note |
| Pin icon | Keep the sticky note always on top |
| ^ | Minimize the sticky note |
| Trash can icon | Delete the sticky note |
| Palette icon | Change the color of the sticky note |
| Binder icon | Copy the content of the sticky note |

![green-note](/green-note.png)

You can drag a note window by either the top or the bottom bar, so moving it never interferes with selecting text.

### Keyboard shortcuts (macOS)

| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|
| `⌘N` | Create a new sticky note |
| `⇧⌘A` | Open the organizer |
| `⌘⏎` | Turn what you typed in quick input into a note |
| `⌘P` | Toggle the pin on the focused note |
| `⌘M` | Minimize the note |
| `⌘W` | Close the note |
| `⌘,` | Open the settings window |
